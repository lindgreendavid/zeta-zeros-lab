# [Finite-Height Rigidity of the Riemann Zeta Zeros](paper.pdf)

**Author:** David Lindgreen · **Date:** October 9, 2026 · **Version:** 0.1.0

**Status:** numerical study. Reproducible from the frozen data and registry in this repository; thresholds were
preregistered before results; **not peer reviewed**. The conclusions are statements about two blocks of
2,000 zeros, not proofs. The analysis code, tests and drafts were produced with substantial AI assistance
(Claude, Anthropic); the author is responsible for the content. A flagged follow-up (not a result) is described in
the paper.
